FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the AquaLink Pro system

How does AquaLink Pro work?

AquaLink Pro uses GSM technology to receive commands via SMS or phone calls. The system is connected to your water line and can control water flow remotely. When you send a command, the device processes it and activates the motorized valve accordingly.

Do I need a smartphone to use AquaLink Pro?

No, you don't need a smartphone. AquaLink Pro works with any basic phone that can make calls or send SMS messages. This makes it accessible to everyone, regardless of technology sophistication.

Is installation difficult?

Installation is very straightforward and typically takes less than 5 minutes. The package includes all necessary fittings and an illustrated installation guide. If you can attach a garden hose, you can install AquaLink Pro.

What happens if there's a power outage?

AquaLink Pro comes with a built-in rechargeable battery backup that provides up to 8 hours of continuous operation during power outages. The valve will maintain its last state until power is restored.

Is there a warranty?

Yes, AquaLink Pro comes with a 1-year manufacturer's warranty covering all defects in materials and workmanship. We also offer extended warranty options for additional peace of mind.
